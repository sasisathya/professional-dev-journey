## If You Get Stuck

### "I don't understand the pattern"
1. Re-read the concept section
2. Watch 5-min YouTube video on the topic
3. Solve problems 1-3 very slowly, step by step
4. Don't move to problem 4 until 1-3 are crystal clear

### "I can't solve the problem"
1. Read problem carefully 2x
2. Identify: What data structure? What approach?
3. Write pseudocode (no actual code)
4. Then implement pseudocode
5. Only if 15+ min stuck: read hint (not solution)

### "I'm falling behind schedule"
1. Skip problem 10 of current pattern (the hardest)
2. Move to next pattern but review previous
3. Don't panic—70% completion > 0% rushing

### "This is too easy/too hard"
- **Too easy?** Jump ahead one phase, but come back if stuck
- **Too hard?** Slow down, do 1 problem per day for this pattern
